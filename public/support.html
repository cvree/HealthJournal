Support

Bellwether is a private symptom journal. No account, nothing sent anywhere unless you switch it on.

Getting help

Email CONTACT_EMAIL with what happened and what you expected instead. If it involves something on screen, a screenshot saves a round trip. Please don't include your journal contents — they won't help, and we would rather not have them.

Common questions

Where is my data?

On your device, in the app's own storage. Not on a server, unless you turned on sync. Deleting the app deletes the journal with it, so export first if you want to keep it.

How do I get my journal out?

Settings → export. You get your full journal as a file you control. This works whether or not sync is on, and it is the recommended way to keep a backup.

Do I need an account?

No. The app works fully with no account at all. An account exists only to make sync possible across devices, and only if you want that.

What does sync actually upload?

Encrypted records, and an email address to sign in with. Your entries are encrypted on your device with a passphrase you choose, before anything is uploaded. That passphrase is never transmitted — which also means that if you lose it, nobody can recover your synced data for you. Keep it somewhere safe.

Is the AI feature reading my journal?

Only if you set it up with your own API key, and only the daily numbers for the window you asked about — no notes, no photos, no name. The app shows you exactly what it is about to send before it sends it. Leave it off and no request is ever made.

Why won't it diagnose anything?

Because it is a tracking tool, not a clinician, and a tool that guesses at conditions would be a worse one. It shows you what you recorded and what tends to occur together. What that means is a conversation to have with whoever is treating you — the appointment report exists to make that conversation easier.

I found a bug.

Please report it to the address above. Include your iOS version and, if you can, the steps that reproduce it.
