Privacy Policy

Health Journal is a private daily health journal. This policy describes what it stores, what can leave your device, and what never does.

Effective EFFECTIVE_DATE · Published by PUBLISHER_NAME

The short version

Your journal is stored on your device and nowhere else, unless you turn on a feature that sends it somewhere. There are two such features, both off until you switch them on, and both explained below.

There is no account required to use the app. There are no analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers, and nothing is ever sold or shared with data brokers. We cannot read your journal.

What is stored on your device

Everything you enter — daily answers, notes, meals, symptoms, measurements, photos, settings, and any custom questions you create — is stored locally in the app's own storage on your device. Nothing in this list is transmitted anywhere as a condition of using the app.

If you set a PIN, it is held separately from the journal itself and is never transmitted.

What can leave your device, and only if you ask

1. Cross-device sync (off by default)

If you turn on sync, your journal is copied to a hosted database so your other devices can read it. Before anything is uploaded, each record's contents are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from a passphrase you choose (PBKDF2-SHA256). That passphrase is never transmitted, and neither is the key.

What the server therefore holds for each record is: the kind of record, its identifier, a timestamp, a device identifier, a deleted flag, and an opaque encrypted blob. A complete copy of the database is a list of dates and unreadable ciphertext. We cannot decrypt it, and neither can the hosting provider.

Sync does require an account, so an email address is stored to identify you and to let you sign in. That email is used for authentication only. Sync is hosted on Supabase, which processes this data on our behalf.

An important limitation, stated plainly: because the app is also delivered over the web, whoever controls the host controls the code that handles your passphrase. This is strong encryption, not a mathematical guarantee against the publisher. No claim of "zero-knowledge" is made.

2. AI-assisted pattern analysis (off by default, your own key)

The app can ask an AI provider to describe possible patterns in your logs. This feature ships with no key and makes no requests until you supply an API key for a provider you choose — currently Google AI Studio, OpenRouter, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure.

When you run an analysis, what is sent is limited to daily numeric and boolean answers plus the labels of the fields you asked about, for the window you selected. Free-text notes, photos, your name, and device information are not included. The app shows you a plain-language summary of exactly what is about to be sent before it sends it.

Your API key is stored under its own key on your device, deliberately outside the journal, so that it cannot travel inside a backup or an export. It is never logged and never transmitted anywhere except to the provider you chose.

Data you send this way is handled under that provider's privacy policy and terms, not this one. You are their customer, using your own credential.

3. Exports and reports you create

When you export a file or generate an appointment report, that file goes wherever you send it. Once it leaves the app it is outside our control and outside this policy.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera only when you actively choose to take a photo for a journal entry. Photos are stored with the rest of your journal on your device, and follow the same rules as everything else: they are included in encrypted sync if you turn sync on, and they are never included in AI analysis.

What this app never does

  • No advertising, and no use of your health data for marketing.
  • No analytics or usage telemetry of any kind.
  • No third-party trackers, no advertising identifiers, no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No sale of personal information, and no sharing with data brokers.
  • No use of your data to train any AI model.

Children

Health Journal is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and your rights

  • See it: your journal is visible in the app, and can be exported in full at any time.
  • Delete it: deleting the app removes the local journal from your device. If you used sync, you can delete synced data and your account from the app's sync settings.
  • Withdraw consent: both sync and AI analysis can be turned off at any time, and turning them off stops all further transmission.
  • Ask us: for anything else, write to CONTACT_EMAIL.

Because the journal lives on your device rather than on our servers, most requests about your data are things you can carry out yourself, immediately, without asking anyone's permission.

Consumer health data

This section applies to residents of jurisdictions with specific consumer health data laws, including Washington State's My Health My Data Act and Nevada SB 370.

  • What consumer health data is collected: none by default. If you enable sync, health-related records are stored in encrypted form that the publisher cannot read, along with your email address. If you enable AI analysis, daily numeric answers are transmitted to the provider you chose.
  • Why: solely to provide the feature you turned on.
  • Sources: you. Nothing is acquired from third parties.
  • Who it is shared with: Supabase, as the hosting processor for encrypted sync; and, for AI analysis only, the AI provider whose key you supplied. No one else.
  • Sale: consumer health data is not sold, and never has been.
  • How to exercise your rights: to access, delete, or withdraw consent, use the app's settings, or write to CONTACT_EMAIL. You may appeal a refused request by replying to our response.

Security

Synced records are encrypted on your device before transmission, and transmitted over TLS. No system is perfectly secure, and the honest summary is above: the design minimises what exists to be breached rather than promising that a breach is impossible.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Changes that materially expand what leaves your device will be surfaced in the app itself, not only here.
